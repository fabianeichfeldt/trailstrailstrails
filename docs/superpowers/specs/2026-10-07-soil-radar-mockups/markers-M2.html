How should a spot show its soil state?

Today's pins encode the spot type by colour (blue trail · green bikepark · magenta dirtpark). That collides with a soil colour scale (green = prime, blue = damp). Each option solves the clash differently. Top: real size on the map. Bottom: 2.2× magnified. Clusters show a soil donut in all variants.

M1 · Soil takes over the pin

In radar mode the whole pin turns soil-coloured with a white soil glyph; Hero Dirt breathes a green glow. Loudest and clearest — but the spot type disappears from the map while the layer is on.

M2 · Soil badge on the pin

The pin stays exactly as today; a small round soil badge with glyph sits on its shoulder (like an app notification badge). Type and soil both readable, Hero Dirt badge glows. Slightly busier.

M3 · Soil fills the pin's core

The white dot inside today's pin becomes a soil-coloured core. Minimal change, very clean — but at real size the core is ~8 px, so dry vs. prime is hard to tell apart, and no glyph fits.
